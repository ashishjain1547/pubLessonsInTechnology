Infinix X6526 (Smart 8 Plus) vs Samsung Galaxy M07

Two entry‑level contenders, two very different philosophies. One bets on raw battery endurance, the other on long‑term software support and a cleaner experience. Here's the breakdown.

The budget phone segment is ruthless, and every rupee matters. The Infinix Smart 8 Plus (model X6526) launched in early 2024 with a massive 6,000mAh battery and aggressive pricing. The Samsung Galaxy M07, quietly released in late 2025, counters with a surprisingly capable MediaTek Helio G99 chip and a promise of six years of updates. Neither is a flagship, but they appeal to very different priorities. Let's compare them side by side.

  • Processor MediaTek Helio G36
  • RAM 4GB (plus 4GB virtual)

  • Front Camera 8MP
  • Battery 6000mAh, 18W fast charging

  • Front Camera 8MP

Where they actually differ

⚡ Performance: Samsung has a clear edge

The Helio G99 inside the Galaxy M07 is a genuinely better chip than the Helio G36 in the Infinix Smart 8 Plus [citation:2][citation:3]. The G99 is built on a more efficient 6nm process and delivers noticeably smoother day‑to‑day performance, better gaming framerates, and more responsive app switching. The Infinix isn't unusable—the G36 handles basic tasks—but the Samsung feels more fluid, especially if you multitask or play casual games. The Infinix attempts to compensate with 4GB of virtual RAM, but virtual memory is no match for a superior silicon foundation [citation:11].

🔋 Battery: Infinix goes big, but Samsung charges faster

On paper, the Infinix’s 6,000mAh battery is a headline feature. It will comfortably last two days for light users, and even heavy users will struggle to kill it in one day [citation:12]. The Galaxy M07’s 5,000mAh cell is more conventional—solid, but not extraordinary. However, Samsung’s 25W fast charging is quicker than Infinix’s 18W, meaning you spend less time tethered to a wall [citation:2][citation:11]. If battery endurance is your absolute top priority, the Infinix wins. If you value a quicker top‑up and a slimmer build, Samsung makes more sense. Notably, the Galaxy M07 ships without a charger in the box, while Infinix includes one [citation:15].

📸 Cameras: Similar hardware, different tuning

Both phones use a 50MP primary sensor and an 8MP selfie shooter. The Samsung adds a 2MP depth sensor for portrait mode, while the Infinix uses an “AI lens” (essentially an auxiliary sensor) and a distinctive quad‑LED ring flash [citation:2][citation:3]. In good light, both produce usable social‑media‑ready shots. Samsung’s processing tends to be slightly more consistent with skin tones and dynamic range, while Infinix leans toward punchier colors. Low‑light performance is mediocre on both—expected at this price. The Infinix’s ring flash is brighter and more even for close‑up shots, but it’s a niche advantage.

💾 Storage & Memory: Infinix gives you more room

The Infinix Smart 8 Plus comes with 128GB of internal storage as standard, double the 64GB on the Galaxy M07 [citation:3][citation:11]. Both support microSD expansion up to 2TB, so the gap is less critical if you’re willing to buy a card. But for users who never touch expandable storage, the Infinix offers twice the breathing room out of the box. That said, the Galaxy M07’s faster processor and cleaner software make its 64GB feel less cramped in daily use.

🛡️ Software & Longevity: Samsung wins by a mile

This is the Galaxy M07’s killer feature: Samsung promises six major OS upgrades and six years of security patches [citation:2][citation:10]. The phone launches with Android 15 and One UI 7, and it will receive updates well into the 2030s. The Infinix Smart 8 Plus ships with Android 13 Go Edition and XOS 13—a lightweight but less polished skin—and its update outlook is far shorter and less certain [citation:3][citation:12]. If you plan to keep your phone for three or more years, the Samsung is the far safer investment. Android Go on the Infinix also means some apps are lighter versions, which can feel restrictive.

📐 Design & Durability: Slimmer Samsung, tougher Infinix?

The Galaxy M07 is remarkably slim at 7.6mm and weighs only 184g, making it one of the most pocketable budget phones around [citation:2][citation:10]. It also carries an IP54 rating for dust and splash resistance—a rare find at this price. The Infinix Smart 8 Plus is thicker (8.95mm) and heavier (204g), largely due to its bigger battery, and lacks any official ingress protection [citation:1][citation:3]. The Infinix’s “Timber Texture” back adds a bit of visual flair, but the Samsung feels more refined in hand.

Verdict Who should buy which?

Choose the Infinix Smart 8 Plus if: You are a light user who prioritizes battery life above all else, want 128GB of storage without paying extra, and need a phone that can go two days between charges. You don’t mind an older Android version and a less powerful chip, and you tend to replace phones every 1–2 years anyway.

Choose the Samsung Galaxy M07 if: You want the best possible performance under ₹7,000–8,000, care about timely software updates and long‑term security, prefer a slim and lightweight phone with splash resistance, and are willing to manage with 64GB (or add a microSD card). The Helio G99 and six years of updates make it the smarter long‑term buy for most people.

Bottom line: The Infinix Smart 8 Plus is a specialist—a battery endurance champion with generous storage. The Samsung Galaxy M07 is a generalist that punches above its weight in performance, software longevity, and design. For most buyers, the Galaxy M07’s superior chip and six‑year update promise make it the more sensible choice, even with the smaller battery and storage. But if you routinely forget to charge your phone and refuse to carry a power bank, the Infinix’s 6,000mAh cell is hard to argue with.
